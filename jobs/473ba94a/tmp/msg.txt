deposco: build get_inventory's product map from the stored catalog

get_inventory pre-fetched the entire Deposco product catalog on every pass just
to read purchaseCost and pack measurements, which dominated the crawl on large
connections (TRA-2554). Those two values now live on each product's
additional_fields, so the pass reads the stored catalog from dynamo instead:

- get_product_and_kit_maps (a full search/item + search/kitHeader walk) becomes
  build_product_and_kit_maps, backed by Product(self).get_all_resources()
- format_inventory_item and format_enterprise_inventory read unit_cost and
  measurements off additional_fields rather than re-deriving them from raw packs
- format_product drops the is_internal branch and its additional_info payload,
  which only existed to feed the pre-fetch

Building the map now either completes or raises, so the "map did not finish
building" zero-fill guard is gone; the database-error and short-feed guards are
unchanged. Products the feed doesn't return still zero-fill as before, and an
inventory item with no stored product still falls back to a synthetic one.

Kits and products not re-crawled since the fields landed carry no cost or
measurements yet and fall back to null cost and empty measurements; they fill in
as the catalog re-crawls.

Verified with yarn test -k deposco --inventory --products (3 passed, 2 write
tests skipped, ledger xfail).
